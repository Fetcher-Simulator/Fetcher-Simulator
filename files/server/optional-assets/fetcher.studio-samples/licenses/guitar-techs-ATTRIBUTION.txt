Fetcher adaptation, 2026: selected sparse pitches from the original player 2
EVH Wolfgang neck-pickup direct input, MIDI-guided attack alignment,
mono averaging, per-root strength normalization, finite tails/end fades,
48-kHz PCM16 conversion and bounded Fetcher bank maps. No harmonic restoration.
One recorded performance per root; no invented velocity layers/round robins.
Source/archive/sample/output identities and exact cuts/gains are recorded in
manifests/guitar-techs-open-v1.json and guitar-techs-palm-v1.json.
No endorsement by the authors or equipment manufacturers is implied.

Package 73 also adapts player 1 Ibanez PF300 bridge-pickup DI with the same
conversion and licensing. Exact cuts and identities are in
manifests/guitar-techs-bridge-open-v1.json and guitar-techs-bridge-palm-v1.json.
The amp input calibration and channel EQ are editable Fetcher processing,
not modifications claimed to be part of the original dataset.

Package 76 pairs two real P1 bridge/P2 neck performances per sparse root in
manifests/guitar-techs-paired-open-v2.json and guitar-techs-paired-palm-v2.json.
V2 identifies physical strings by their E/A/D/G/B/e track names, not MIDI
channels (P1 shares channel zero). It selects the smallest available fret at
unchanged concert pitch, excludes P1's velocity-10 pickup annotations, and
retains exact source cuts/hashes for each take. These are actual alternate
recordings, not synthesized variations. Existing v1 banks retain their bytes.
