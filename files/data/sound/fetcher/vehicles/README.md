- `pickup_gravel_skid.ogg` - `auto car or van stop brake skid gravel short.flac` by kyles, CC0
  - https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/637161/

The files included here are derived from the freely streamable Ogg Vorbis previews supplied by Freesound. The gravel rolling loop was converted to mono for positional 3D playback. They may be edited, redistributed, and used commercially under CC0.
